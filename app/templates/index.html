How a group deal works

  1. Commit with $15

    Join a building's committed group with a refundable $15 deposit. It shows the leasing office you're serious. Leave the group and you forfeit it.

  2. Fill the windows

    Every renter who commits lights a window. Invite friends to hit the target before the group's due date. Miss it and everyone gets their $15 back.

  3. Get the offer

    If the manager is on aptapt, they make the group an offer. If not, your group sets its own ask and we pitch it to them.

  4. Reserve and sign

    When the deal unlocks, you have 72 hours to reserve a unit. Sign your lease and your $15 comes back.
